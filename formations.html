Formations

Where your squads actually stand, drawn from the game's own formation table. Every army fights as 16 squads: 4 infantry, 4 ranged, 4 cavalry and 4 siege.

  • Infantry
  • Ranged
  • Cavalry
  • Siege

Infantry Phalanx

4 infantry squads in one wide front line. Cav sits on the outer flanks behind them, ranged at the back.

Ranged Phalanx

4 ranged squads in front. Infantry on the flanks just behind, cav at the back.

Cavalry Phalanx

4 cav squads in front. Ranged on the flanks behind, infantry at the back.

Infantry Wedge

Infantry stacked 2-deep in the centre, so narrower than Inf Phalanx. Ranged on the flanks, cav at the back.

Ranged Wedge

Ranged stacked 2-deep in the centre. Cav on the wide flanks, infantry at the back.

Cavalry Wedge

Cav stacked 2-deep in the centre. Infantry on the wide flanks, ranged at the back.

There's no special Wonder mode. Wonder battles run on the same simulator as every other fight; Wonders only add stat bonuses for holding them and for titles. game files

In Wonders the comp often matters more than the formation name. A 055 sits in "Infantry Wedge" with only 40–50K infantry, so its real front is ranged on the flanks with cav behind. Read the troop numbers, not the formation label.

What the logs say about switching

  • Cav Phalanx into a Range Phalanx costs most of your cav, but it isn't an automatic loss: it won twice in old reports and lost narrowly today. log 17 log 20 log 2
  • Switch late. If they scout you sitting in Cav Phalanx, a smart lead comes in Range Phalanx.
  • Only call off the final rally numbers. A scout of a half-filled rally got us the wrong call. log 2

Common mistakes

  • Running Cav Phalanx without enough infantry behind it, into an Infantry Phalanx.
  • Not switching after being scouted.
  • Reading the formation name instead of the troop numbers.
  • Only one ranged hero in Range Phalanx. Your middle ranged squad ends up out of line and gets bypassed or picked off; three is just as bad. Use exactly two. Crystal Briefs
  • Leaving formations unbound in your hotkeys. Range Wedge is the one people forget, and the 208 needs it.
