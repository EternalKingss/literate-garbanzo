Familiars by formation

  • Range Phalanx: Snow Beast, Blackwing or Queen Bee, Jade Wyrm, Hell Drider
  • Cav Phalanx: Gargantua, Frostwing, Saberfang, Jade Wyrm, Hell Drider
  • Inf Phalanx: Bon Appeti, Huey Hops, Hoarder, Jade Wyrm, Hell Drider

Watch enemy Jade Wyrms: in an even fight, a 9% damage cut can decide it. Run your own to cancel it.

Hero setups by formation

  • Range Phalanx: 2 ranged heroes. 1 infantry, 2 ranged, 2 cavalry is the old site's pick.
  • Inf Phalanx: 2 infantry, 2 ranged, 1 cavalry; for a 1433, 4–5 infantry heroes.
  • 055: ranged and cavalry heroes, at most one infantry.

Utility familiars

Listed by the old site; not checked against the game.

  • Goblin (Pact 3): gold every 24 hours
  • Gemming Gremlin (Pact 3): gems every 2 days
  • Hoarder (Pact 4): random jewels every 3 days
  • Mecha Trojan (Pact 4): extra rally capacity
  • Grim Reaper (Pact 4): kills high-tier troops in an enemy's barracks
  • Aquiris (Pact 1): stamina every 12 hours
  • Territe (Pact 1): faster merging
