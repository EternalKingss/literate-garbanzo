Six rules from 20 logged fights

  • Read numbers, not names

    A 055 sits in "Infantry Wedge" with 40K infantry. The biggest stack tells you what's really in front.

  • Counter their biggest stack

    Their infantry: your cav in front. Their ranged: your infantry in front. Their cav: your ranged in front.

  • Mirrors go to cav

    Range Phalanx vs Range Phalanx: more cav wins. Cav counts about twice as much as ranged.

  • Never sit with no infantry

    Hold 1.4M+ infantry, 1.1M+ ranged, 1.6M+ cav, full cap.

  • Call off final numbers

    Half-filled rallies change. Switch late, after the last scout.

  • Check the rally screen

    Fillers drift toward infantry. Your 668 is useless if it lands as a 758.

The evidence for each rule is on Wonder rules, and every fight is in the battle log. Troop counts for each comp are on Comps.
