Read a scout, pick the formation.

Type the troop totals from a scout or a rally screen. You get the comp, what to sit in if they hit you, and what to send if you hit them.

Their troops

Holder's march plus all allied troops. 50k, 1.3m and 1,300,000 all work. Fill two and the third is worked out from the rally cap.

Enter at least two of their troop totals, then press Get the call.
Your troops (optional)

Leave empty to get a comp to send. Fill them in to check a mirror and your holding minimums.

Six rules from 32 logged fights

  • Read numbers, not names

    A 055 sits in "Infantry Wedge" with 40K infantry. The biggest stack tells you what's really in front.

  • Counter their biggest stack

    Their cav: ranged in front. Their ranged: infantry in front. Their infantry: Ranged Phalanx, or cav in front if they have under 1M cav.

  • Mirrors: guard, lead, infantry

    Ranged-heavy needs 3+ parts cav and about 700K more ranged. Otherwise more infantry wins. Called 10 of 12 logged mirrors.

  • 055: check the buffer

    Sending: 50–60K infantry, not 40K. Holding: Ranged Phalanx against 40K. Against 50K+, Infantry Phalanx if you're stronger, else pull.

  • Call off final numbers

    Half-filled rallies change. Switch late, after the last scout.

  • Check the rally screen

    Fillers drift toward infantry. Your 668 is useless if it lands as a 758.

The evidence for each rule is on Wonder rules, and every fight is in the battle log. Troop counts for each comp are on Comps.
