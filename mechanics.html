The engine

A fight is a 40-second simulation between squads, not between single troops. Once you know the squad rules, odd reports start making sense. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs

  • Every troop type makes exactly 4 squads, each a quarter of that type. Each tier is split evenly across the 4. Inside a squad the lowest tier dies first.
  • A squad attacks one enemy squad at a time, starting with the closest. It only switches target when that squad is completely dead. Who switches onto whom (retargeting) decides most Wonder fights.
  • Cav moves fastest, ranged slowest. Tier doesn't change battle speed.
  • T5 is about 1.33× T4. A big T5 gap beats a correct formation; in his Dragon Arena reviews the side with far more T5 kept winning "countered" fights. Crystal Briefs
  • Morale decides nothing. The bar in the movie is drawn backwards from the final losses. Crystal Briefs
  • The battle movie is accurate. Watch it to see which squad hit which. That's how you tell why a fight went wrong.

Formations in mixed fights

A formation only sets where your squads start, so it decides who takes damage first. It doesn't buff anything. Crystal Briefs

  • Putting your counter troop in front is the weak way to counter. With mixed comps, Ranged Phalanx beats Infantry Phalanx, Infantry Phalanx beats Cav Phalanx, and Cav Phalanx beats Ranged Phalanx. The winner is the side whose back line keeps hitting at full strength.
  • Cav Phalanx into Ranged Phalanx is the weakest of the three. Ranged gets about three full volleys into the cav before the infantry arrives. Our logs agree. A cav front held or broke even against about 1.1M ranged log 2 log 17 log 20 log 23. It lost twice to a 2.2M ranged stack log 25 log 26.
  • Don't trust the drill on the formation page. It changes the troop split when you change formation, so it can't compare formations. Crystal Briefs
  • Cav Wedge is never the best pick for a 424, except maybe against Infantry Phalanx. It loses to Ranged Phalanx because its infantry can only reach the two outer ranged squads. Crystal Briefs
  • Ranged Wedge: never take a 055 in it. It's the formation that blunts a 208. Against a cav blast already sitting Ranged Phalanx, stay in Ranged Phalanx: Ranged Wedge lets the cav reach your infantry. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs

Range Phalanx mirrors

Both sides sit Ranged Phalanx. The two outer ranged squads on each side die first, then the middle ones. What happens after that decides the fight.

  • 424 vs 424: the side whose ranged dies first usually wins. Once both fronts are gone, the targeting sends the other side's back line onto the winner's cav, and onto the loser's infantry. This is how weaker accounts beat stronger ones, and why more attack can lose you a mirror. The fix for the stronger side is a 1-2-2 hero lineup or sitting Cav Phalanx instead. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs
  • 442 vs 424: results swing to big wins or big losses, rarely close. The 442 wins big if the 424's ranged is dead before half the 442's ranged is. You can spot it in the report: about half the 442's ranged survives. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs Logs 7 and 31 look exactly like this: a ranged-heavy rally won both, once with 747K less cav. log 7 log 31
  • A 442 still needs its cav. After it wins the ranged trade, its cav is what keeps the enemy off its infantry. Crystal Briefs Our 8-11-1 rallies, with under 400K cav, lost twice into a 758. log 5 log 6

The old cav rule is retired. "More cav wins a mirror" was fitted to our first 6 mirrors. Across all 12 mirrors with numbers it called 6. "More infantry wins" called 5. "Whoever's ranged dies first" called 7. Troop counts alone don't decide mirrors; heroes, familiars and stats do. The working rule that replaced it (cav guard, ranged lead, then infantry) is on Wonder rules.

Heroes

A hero's stats matter less than its tactical skill. The skill hits for double a normal squad attack about four times a fight, and it scales with the squad's size. In his test a hero with no useful stats added about 20% damage, while 20 army attack from another hero added about 1.5%. The hero's troop type has to match the squad or the skill never fires. Crystal Briefs

A squad with a hero stands one step further back, because the skill reaches further than a normal attack. That shifts who reaches whom, so the number of heroes of each type changes how a fight plays out. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs

Stats & research

  • Attack and HP: raise both. They work together. More attack can lose a 424 Ranged Phalanx mirror, because you kill their ranged first. Stat changes also move when familiars fire. Crystal Briefs
  • Multiplying stats beat adding stats. In order: counter stats (research, Chronicler, Jade Wyrm), then the enemy-debuff research (it hits all their stats), then T5. For Wonders, the coalition army-size research adds 1M to your rally. Crystal Briefs
  • Talents: max army attack and HP, then spread the rest evenly across the individual attacks. A 208 maxes infantry and cav attack. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs
  • Comp before stat spread. On a 424, keep your cav stat within about 100 of your ranged. Crystal Briefs

Comp playbooks

424 types (857, 758, 947, 749, 659, 848)

Beat most comps at your level and every formation works except Cav Wedge. If you don't know what's coming, Ranged Phalanx. Heroes 1-2-2; infantry and cav pact-5 familiars; Cav Shield if you'll sit Cav Phalanx. Weak spots: 442s in Ranged Phalanx, and 055s or 208s when you run 2 infantry heroes. Crystal Briefs

442 types (884, 974, 875, 992, 794, 785)

Sit Ranged Phalanx about 90% of the time; switch to Ranged Wedge if a strong 208 is coming. Ranged Shield always. Infantry-heavy comps are their natural counter. Crystal Briefs

  • Against 424s in Ranged Phalanx: plain 884 is the best trigger for the 442 win. 974 is close, 875 lost narrowly. Crystal Briefs
  • Against Infantry Phalanx: 875 (more cav) is best. Against Cav Phalanx: 884, and 884 in Infantry Phalanx crushes it. Crystal Briefs
  • 974 beats 992 and plain 884. Crystal Briefs The cav in a 992 shields its infantry after the win. Crystal Briefs

055

  • Target: any 424 with 2 infantry heroes. Cav-heavy 424s are ideal. Crystal Briefs
  • Infantry buffer: 40–60K T4 or better. About 20K or about 100K fails. Use less against a weaker ranged front. Our logs used 40–50K. Crystal Briefs
  • Formation: Infantry Wedge only. Heroes 0-4-1. Familiars: ranged pact-5s, Cav Shield, cav stacker. Gear: ranged attack and cav HP first.
  • Did it work? About half their cav dead means yes. In our logs 12 and 15 the defender's cav was barely touched, so those 055s never got through. log 12 log 15
  • Against Hell Drider a cav-heavy 055 such as 0-9-11 still wins. Crystal Briefs

208

Target: infantry-heavy 424s (947, 1046) in Ranged Phalanx. The 208 attacks in Ranged Wedge. Its two front cav squads must survive; if the report shows damage on the 208's infantry, it failed. Riskier against thick ranged. A 307 beats 442s more easily but is weaker against Ranged Wedge. Familiars: 2 cav pact-5, cav stacker, Cav Shield, plus Jade Wyrm or Griffin. Crystal Briefs

Ranged-cav comps (4-8-8, 4-10-6, our 5-10-4)

Weak against infantry-heavy 424s, fine against cav-heavy ones. 4-10-6 is the most stable against Ranged Phalanx, but Ranged Wedge kills it. Crystal Briefs Our 5-10-4 beat a Cav Phalanx twice. log 25 log 26

Why each comp wins

To call a matchup nobody has logged, check how each comp wins and whether the other comp can stop that from happening. Five mechanics cover almost everything:

  1. The ranged trade. In Ranged Phalanx the two ranged fronts kill each other first. Your infantry kills their ranged; their infantry kills yours. More infantry means their ranged dies faster; more ranged means yours lasts longer. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs
  2. The 442 win. A ranged-heavy comp wins big if the other side's ranged is dead before half of its own is. Its surviving ranged then shoots their cav, and their cav is stuck fighting the troop that counters it. Needs lots of ranged and enough infantry. Crystal Briefs log 7 log 31
  3. The cav guard. After a 442 wins the ranged trade, its cav is what keeps their infantry and cav off its own infantry. Under about 2 parts of cav, that guard breaks. Crystal Briefs log 5 log 6
  4. Back-line formations. Ranged Phalanx beats Infantry Phalanx, Infantry Phalanx beats Cav Phalanx, Cav Phalanx beats Ranged Phalanx. The winner's back line hits at full strength while it eats the other side's front. Cav into ranged is the weakest of the three. Crystal Briefs
  5. Breaking through. 055 and 208 get past a ranged front to the infantry and cav behind it. A 055 needs a defender with 2 infantry heroes; a 208 needs thin ranged and an infantry-heavy target. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs
