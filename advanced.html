Range Phalanx mirrors: cav decides

When both sides sit Range Phalanx, both ranged fronts die first. What's left is cav against whatever the other side has behind. In the logs, the side with more cav won every time unless the other side brought a much bigger ranged stack.

Rule of thumb: work out your cav gap and your ranged gap against them. Cav counts about twice as much as ranged. If cav gap + half the ranged gap is negative, don't hit. This weighting is a rough fit to the six fights below, not a formula from the game.

The 055, in numbers

  • Infantry: the lead sends 40–50K and nobody else sends any. Every logged 055 used 40K or 50K.
  • Ranged limit: the old "avoid over 1.1M ranged" is too strict. Into about 1.76M ranged with under 1M cav it traded even twice. log 8 log 14
  • Into 758/857 Range Phalanx: stats decide. Not stronger: defender wins log 12 log 15. Clearly stronger: won by 1.1M log 10.
  • As a garrison: beat an 875 from a stronger account. log 9
  • The old "2 infantry heroes" rule contradicted itself (one page said it helps you, another said avoid). Dropped until someone tests it.

Comp drift and bad calls

  • Fillers drift toward infantry. Rallies sent as 668 landed as 7.5-5-7.5 twice. Check the rally screen percentages before you launch. log 1 log 3
  • Call off final numbers only. Scouting a rally that's still filling gave the wrong formation. log 2
  • Pull when outmatched. If the target has more cav than you in a mirror, skip it or let another rally soften it first.

Community theory we haven't tested

These come from the old site. They might be right. None of today's reports confirm or rule them out.

  • Two ranged heroes in Range Phalanx. With one, your middle ranged squad sits forward and gets picked off; with zero or three it's said to be worse.
  • Hero reach. A hero's tactical skill reaches slightly further than a plain squad attack, so squads with heroes hold back a step.
  • 442 vs 424 Range Phalanx. If the 424's ranged dies before half the 442's ranged, the 442 wins; if the 424's ranged is tanky, the 424 wins by a lot. 974, 992, 794 and 983 are 442 variants built around this.
  • Attack vs HP. HP cuts your losses, attack raises kills. More attack can sometimes make a matchup worse, because it changes when familiars fire.
