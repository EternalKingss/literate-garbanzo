Range Phalanx mirrors: shape, not cav

The old rule here said more cav wins a mirror. It held for our first 6 mirrors and then missed all 6 new ones. The full check is on How fights work.

Most promising so far: a ranged-heavy rally that keeps about 0.9M cav (794, 884), sent into a 424-type sitter. It won twice with less cav and lost once by 61K. log 7 log 31 log 13 Crystal Briefs With almost no cav it lost twice. log 5 log 6 Small sample: a lead, not a rule.

  • Otherwise it's a toss-up on numbers. Heroes, familiars and stats decide it. Hit only if your lead is clearly stronger.
  • Sit with exactly 2 ranged heroes. Crystal Briefs
  • Refill your ranged after every mirror. It's always gone.

Garrison minimums

Never sit with no infantry. In log 11 we had more cav and more ranged than the attacker and lost by 1.46M, because his infantry hit our ranged with nothing in the way. log 11

The 055, in numbers

  • Infantry: the lead sends 40–50K and nobody else sends any. Every logged 055 used 40K or 50K.
  • Ranged limit: the old "avoid over 1.1M ranged" is too strict. Into about 1.76M ranged with under 1M cav it traded even twice. log 8 log 14
  • Into 758/857 Range Phalanx: stats decide. Not stronger: defender wins log 12 log 15. Clearly stronger: won by 1.1M log 10.
  • As a garrison: beat an 875 from a stronger account. log 9
  • Heroes: the 055 runs 4 ranged + 1 cav and no infantry hero. A defender with 2 infantry heroes is what it feeds on; holding with 1 limits it to one cav squad. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs
  • Holding against one: Ranged Phalanx log 12 log 15 log 16 or Infantry Phalanx log 29. Never Ranged Wedge.

Comp drift and bad calls

  • Fillers drift toward infantry. Rallies sent as 668 landed as 7.5-5-7.5 twice. Check the rally screen percentages before you launch. log 1 log 3
  • Call off final numbers only. Scouting a rally that's still filling gave the wrong formation. log 2
  • Pull when outmatched. A big stat or T5 gap beats the right formation. Skip a stronger lead in a mirror, or let another rally soften it first. Crystal Briefs

Most of today's fights were between accounts with similar gear, so formation and comp decided them. A clearly stronger lead flips close matchups: the same 055-into-758 matchup lost twice and then won by 1.1M with the strongest lead. log 10 log 12 log 15
