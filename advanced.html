The core idea

Every Wonder fight has two phases. Phase 1, the front trade: the fronts meet and each side's troops hit the troop they counter. Whatever is still standing goes into phase 2, where the targeting sends each side's back line onto whatever the other side has left. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs

You win by doing two things: win the front trade, and still have a guard for phase 2. Every comp is a different way of doing that:

  • 442s win phase 1 with sheer ranged, and their cav guards their infantry in phase 2.
  • 055s skip the front trade: they break through at the edges and hit your cav directly.
  • 208s send a little infantry to win the trade against your ranged while their cav runs straight for phase 2.
  • A garrison with no infantry can't fight phase 1 at all.

Every rule below is one of these.

Holding: what to sit in

Proven

Against a 055: Ranged or Infantry Phalanx. Never Ranged Wedge.

Sit Ranged Phalanx with only 1 infantry hero, or Infantry Phalanx.

Why: A 055 wins by breaking your ranged front at the edges and walking its ranged onto your cav. In Infantry Phalanx your front is infantry, the one troop its ranged can't beat, so it never gets through. In Ranged Phalanx it needs your 2 infantry heroes to reach both cav squads; with 1, it reaches one. Ranged Wedge hands it the edges.

Held in Ranged Phalanx log 12 log 15 log 16 and Infantry Phalanx log 29; in log 29 your cav lost only 13%. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs

Strong

Cav in front only against a small ranged stack.

Cav Phalanx holds against about 1.1M ranged. Against 2M+ ranged, don't.

Why: Their ranged gets about three full volleys into your cav before your infantry reaches it. Each volley scales with how much ranged they have, so a 2M stack shreds the cav in phase 1.

Won or even at about 1.1M log 2 log 17 log 20 log 23; lost at 2.2M twice log 25 log 26. Crystal Briefs

Why: Infantry is the counter to ranged, so you win phase 1, and a 442 only has about 4 parts of cav to punish your infantry front. A cav front would face their 2M ranged (see above).

Ranged Phalanx mirrors

Proven

A ranged-heavy comp needs its cav guard.

Keep at least 3 of 20 parts in cav (about 650K). Never send an 8-11-1.

Why: A 442 wins the ranged trade, then the other side's infantry and cav go looking for targets. Its cav is what stands in the way. Without it, they run straight into its infantry.

8-11-1 lost twice into a 758 log 5 log 6; a 1262 with 385K cav lost by 527K log 4. Crystal Briefs

Working

A 442 needs a big ranged lead.

A ranged-heavy rally wins when it brings about 700K more ranged than the target. Under that, the target wins.

Why: The 442 win only happens if their ranged is dead before half of yours is. That takes a big ranged surplus plus enough infantry; a small surplus just turns it into a slugging match the 424 wins.

Won with +717K and +907K log 7 log 31; lost with +527K log 13. Crystal Briefs

Working

424 against 424: more infantry wins.

When neither side is ranged-heavy, the side with more infantry usually wins. Within about 100K, it's stats and heroes.

Why: With the ranged fronts gone, infantry is the troop that soaks the other side's back line and keeps killing. Exception so far: a 222K cav edge beat a 250K infantry edge in log 3.

Fits logs 1, 21, 24, 27 and 28; misses log 3, and log 22 was a 36K coin flip. Crystal Briefs adds that in a true even mirror, whoever's ranged dies first can flip it. Crystal Briefs

The 055

Proven

Infantry buffer: 50–60K, T4 or better. Not 40K.

Only the lead sends infantry: 50–60K at T4 or above. Nobody else sends any.

Why: The buffer is a timer. At the start every enemy squad aims at the closest 055 squads, which is the infantry at the tip. It stays locked on until that squad dies. The buffer has to soak their opening, including their infantry heroes' big skill hits, and die at the right moment so their squads retarget where the 055 wants. Die too early and the 055 never reaches their cav. Last too long and it never does either: in his test, 100K left their cav completely untouched. How long it lasts depends on how hard their ranged hits, and today's top accounts hit far harder than his 2022 tests. That's why our 40K 055s failed and 50K got through.

40K into Ranged Phalanx lost twice and barely touched their cav (0 and 11%) log 12 log 15. 50K won by 1.1M log 10 and traded even into 1.77M ranged, reaching 35% of their cav log 14. Crystal Briefs Crystal Briefs

Proven

Who to hit with a 055.

A 424 sitting Ranged Phalanx with 2 infantry heroes. Not an Infantry Phalanx sitter.

Why: Its ranged has to break their ranged front to reach the cav. Against an infantry front it's fighting its counter, so it never gets through. If you know they sit Infantry Phalanx, Crystal Briefs sends the 055 in Ranged Phalanx instead.

Into Infantry Phalanx it lost by 178K and reached 13% of their cav log 29. Crystal Briefs

Theory

How to build it.

Infantry Wedge. Heroes: 4 ranged, 1 cav, no infantry hero. Familiars: ranged pact-5s, Cav Shield, cav stacker. Gear: ranged attack and cav HP first. Did it work? About half their cav dead means yes.

Why: Ranged heroes speed up breaking their front. Your cav ends up as the front line in Infantry Wedge, so it needs HP and the shield. An infantry hero breaks the targeting the buffer sets up.

The 208

Theory

Who to hit with a 208, and how.

Hit infantry-heavy 424s (947, 1046 type) sitting Ranged Phalanx with about 1.1M ranged or less. Send it in Ranged Wedge.

Why: Each of its infantry squads takes one of their ranged squads, which is its counter, while the two front cav squads go past the ranged to reach their infantry. Its infantry must survive the whole fight; any damage on the 208's infantry in the report means it failed. Thick ranged means the infantry can't finish in time. Ranged Wedge on their side blocks the cav, and a 442's ranged is too much.

Garrison

Strong

Never hold with no infantry.

Hold 1.4M+ infantry, 1.1M+ ranged, 1.6M+ cav, at full cap.

Why: Infantry is your only answer to their ranged. With none, their ranged survives the front trade untouched and shoots your cav for the whole fight, while their infantry eats your ranged. Ranged is your front in Ranged Phalanx. Cav is your phase-2 guard.

More cav and ranged than the attacker, no infantry, lost by 1.46M log 11.

Proven

A big stat or T5 gap beats the right formation.

Don't take or send a hit into a much stronger lead and expect the counter to save you.

Why: Each squad's strength is stats × tier × count. A big enough gap wins the front trade no matter how it's set up.

The same 055-into-758 matchup lost twice and then won by 1.1M with the strongest lead log 10 log 12 log 15. Crystal Briefs

Theory

Heroes set where your squads stand.

Exactly 2 ranged heroes whenever you sit Ranged Phalanx. 1-2-2 on a 424.

Why: A squad with a hero stands one step back, which changes who reaches whom. With 1 or 3 ranged heroes your middle ranged squads end up out of line.
